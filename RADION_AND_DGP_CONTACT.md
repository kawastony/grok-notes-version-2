# Radion stabilization and DGP — contact only

Under frozen claims: relative Σ_b/operator structure is stronger; absolute scale calibrated or Pathway-1 bridged; μ pure-geometry derivation open. RS/DGP here are **context**, not TAFA theorems.

---

## Radion stabilization (RS)

**Problem:** In RS1 the inter-brane distance (modulus / radion) is not fixed by the Einstein–Hilbert + brane-tension action alone; without a potential the radius can run and the hierarchy is unstable.

**Classic fix — Goldberger–Wise:** bulk scalar with bulk mass and brane potentials generates a radion potential. Mild parameter choices stabilize k r_c ~ 12 (hierarchy without new large ratios). Radion mass typically O(TeV); couplings set by the IR scale.

**Other mechanisms:** Casimir energy from bulk fields; confining bulk gauge sector (brane tension vs Casimir pressure); generalizations with backreaction.

### Contact with TAFA / μ no-go

| RS radion | TAFA reading |
|-----------|--------------|
| Stabilized length 1/k or r_c | **Dimensionful input** the absolute-scale no-go requires |
| Radion mass / VEV | Possible analog of μ or related IR scale |
| Unstabilized modulus | Geometry alone does not fix SI scales — same lesson as our no-go |

**Limit:** Goldberger–Wise is not a derivation of galactic a_eff or of μ in the TAFA papers. It shows *how* a 5D theory can supply the missing length; it does not compute that length for SPARC.

## DGP gravity (alternative modified-gravity branch)

**Setup:** 4D brane in 5D Minkowski with induced Einstein–Hilbert term on the brane. Crossover scale r_c marks transition from 4D to 5D gravity.

**Branches:**
- **Self-accelerating:** late acceleration without Λ; historically interesting; **ghost** in linear theory; strongly constrained / disfavored by CMB+SN+lensing (~4–5σ class in literature).
- **Normal:** no self-acceleration; needs tension/Λ-like term; modified growth.

### Contact with TAFA

| DGP | TAFA |
|-----|------|
| IR modification of gravity | Different target (cosmic acceleration vs galactic a_eff) |
| r_c as single scale | Again: one external length |
| Self-accelerating ghost / data tension | Caution against “geometry alone ⇒ acceleration” without stability checks |

TAFA’s Pathway 1 uses ρ_Λ explicitly (bridged), not DGP self-acceleration. Do not identify TAFA with DGP.

## One-line takeaway

Radion stabilization illustrates the **necessary external (or bulk-field) scale** behind any warped hierarchy; DGP shows an IR-gravity alternative that is observationally strained on the self-accelerating branch. Both reinforce the framework’s no-go and bridge posture; neither closes μ from pure cone geometry.
