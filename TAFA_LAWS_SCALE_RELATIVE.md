# TAFA laws rewritten with scale-relative pause

**Status:** Working law set. Meta-law is prior; four laws hold *within any fixed scale*. Absolute scale / one-input boundary unchanged.

---

## Meta-Law (prior to the four)

### Meta-Law of Relative Phase Resolution

Phase roles are scale-relative. Every system participates in both active and pause phases. What counts as active phase, pause phase, or bridge depends on the observational level and on neighboring bridge structure. A lower bridge can function as a pause relative to a higher bridge. At larger scales phases may appear singular; at smaller scales they multiply, nest, and become relative.

The four laws below are applied **after** a scale (or bridge level) has been chosen.

---

## The four laws (within a chosen scale)

### Law 1 — Identity selection at the pause

At the pause phase appropriate to the chosen scale, the system selects and locks the identity that remains invariant through subsequent deformation at that scale.

- Pause is not only preservation; it is **selection and commitment**.
- Which pause is the dominant gate is scale-dependent (Meta-Law).
- Micro: many local selection events. Galactic: typically one dominant r_p-class pause. Cosmic: often one major pivot.

### Law 2 — Propagation of the chosen identity

What is chosen at the pause unfolds through the medium as sourced deformation (transport, flow, residual response, soft-mode weight motion, …).

- Propagation is downstream of identity selection.
- At lower scales, propagation events can nest inside a single higher-scale active stretch.

### Law 3 — Exchange and sector balance

The chosen identity generates compensating balances across sectors (compressive/tensile, DM-like/DE-like, defect/anti-defect, local/cosmic, …).

- Exchange is the relational cost of the chosen orientation.
- Endpoint split Δ around a fixed midpoint is one formal image of exchange (Case A / Case B).

### Law 4 — Bridge readout

The large-scale (or next-scale) observable is the asymptotic readout of the identity selected at the pause — possibly after projection by a geometric factor (e.g. 1/Q) or tension factor (Λ_*^2).

- A bridge at this scale may appear as a pause relative to a higher scale (Meta-Law).
- Pathway 1 is a bridge readout from cosmic ω_* to galactic a_eff; r_p is a galactic pause that can bridge cosmic regimes.

## Operational sequence (fixed scale)

```text
1. Identify the scale / bridge level
2. Locate the pause(s) at that scale
3. Law 1: identity selected and locked
4. Law 2: identity propagates
5. Law 3: sectors exchange / balance
6. Law 4: observables read out the chosen identity
7. (Optional) Re-embed: this scale’s bridge may be pause-like for a higher scale
```
