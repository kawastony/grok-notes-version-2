## 2. Nested phase resolution — formal sketch

Label levels by an index n (higher n = larger scale):

- At level n: phases {active_n, pause_n} and bridges B_n that connect neighboring phases or sectors.
- A structure that is a **bridge B_n** at level n can function as a **pause_{n+1}** relative to level n+1 when it is the still interval that mediates two higher-order active stretches.
- Conversely, a single active stretch at level n+1 can contain many (active, pause, bridge) cycles at level n.

```text
Level n+1 (e.g. cosmic):
  [======== ACTIVE ========]  PAUSE  [======== ACTIVE ========]
              │                  │
              │                  └── single coarse identity-selection gate
              │
Level n (e.g. galactic):
  active | pause(r_p) | active     ← nested inside one cosmic active/pause block

Level n-1 (e.g. micro):
  a|p|a|p|a|p|…                ← dense lattice of local selections and bridges
```

**Relative pause rule:**  
If process P sits between two higher-order phases and does not itself drive the higher dynamics, P is pause-like *relative to that higher frame*, even if P is an active bridge in its native frame.

## 4. Why cosmic looks simple and micro looks complex

- **Coarse-graining:** Many micro pauses average into one effective meso pause; many meso bridges average into one cosmic pivot.
- **Selection hierarchy:** Dominant identity-choice at large scale constrains which local orientations are available; local pauses still select *within* that constraint.
- **Equal-interval motif:** Natural at micro (lattice spacing, spectral steps); not required to appear as equal intervals at cosmic scale — only as alternation of roles.

This matches the corpus: cosmic papers emphasize one pause phase; microscopic/quantum notes emphasize many bridges and nested pauses.

---

## 5. Identity selection under nesting

- At each resolved pause, identity can be selected *at that scale*.
- Higher-scale selection sets the envelope; lower-scale selection fills in orientation detail.
- A system can therefore be “free” locally (many micro pauses) while “bound” by a coarse cosmic or galactic identity already chosen.
- Suffering / consequence intensity in the active phase can track how tightly the chosen identity binds the system — interpretive layer only.

## 6. What this does not do

- Does not derive μ_* or ω_* from pure geometry alone.
- Does not force (w₀, wₐ) or 12.1 kpc into the zero-parameter core.
- Does not replace hierarchical SPARC or cosmology tests.
- Does not claim every local fluctuation is a free-will event.

It **does** supply a coherent grammar for why the same framework looks sparse at cosmic scale and dense at microscopic scale.
