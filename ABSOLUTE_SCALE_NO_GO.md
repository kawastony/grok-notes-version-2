# Absolute scale no-go lemma (pure cone geometry)

## Lemma

**Dimensionless cone geometry plus O(1) geometric factors cannot produce an SI acceleration without at least one external dimensionful scale.**

## Internal inventory (scale-free or dimensionless)

| Quantity | Character |
|----------|-----------|
| Angles ψ₁, ψ₂ (35°, 70°) | Dimensionless |
| Q=√2, φ, R_cone=√6/φ | Dimensionless O(1) |
| κ₀=0.394, C_ang | Dimensionless O(1) |
| ν₀ (Legendre root) | Dimensionless |
| γ, Δ_X, n_eff, Δ_μ | Dimensionless |
| Σ_b/Σ_ref | Dimensionless ratio |

Any product or function of these alone is dimensionless (or a pure number). Acceleration has units L/T².

## What is required

At least one of:
- μ or Λ_* with SI units (from UV, lattice spacing, wall thickness, …),
- ρ_Λ or H₀ (Pathway 1 class),
- an empirical a_eff calibration (SPARC median).

## Corollary

Relative structure laws (Σ_b^γ, residual cleanup, Q-fixed fits) **do not** depend on closing the absolute scale. The no-go targets only pure-geometry derivation of SI a_eff.
