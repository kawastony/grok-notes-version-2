## 1. Goldberger–Wise (GW) stabilization

### Setup
Add a bulk scalar Φ to RS1 with bulk mass m and brane potentials that prefer different VEVs v_h (UV/hidden) and v_v (IR/visible).

### Effective radion potential (standard form)
With ε ≈ m²/(4k²) ≪ 1 and large k r_c:
\[
V_{\rm GW}(r_c) \simeq k\varepsilon v_h^2 + 4k e^{-4k r_c\pi}(v_v - v_h e^{-\varepsilon k r_c\pi})^2\Bigl(1+\frac{\varepsilon}{4}\Bigr) - \cdots
\]
Minimum (leading):
\[
k r_c \approx \frac{4}{\pi}\frac{k^2}{m^2}\ln\frac{v_h}{v_v}.
\]
Example: v_h/v_v = 1.5, m/k = 0.2 → k r_c ≈ 12 — hierarchy without large parameter ratios.

### Outputs
- Stabilized inter-brane distance (the missing dimensionful modulus).
- Radion mass typically set by the IR scale (often O(TeV) in particle RS); couplings ~1/TeV to SM fields on the IR brane.
- Backreaction on the metric is often neglected at leading order; controlled treatments exist.

### TAFA contact
GW is the textbook answer to “geometry alone does not fix the radius.” It supplies the external (or bulk-field) scale our absolute-scale no-go requires. **It does not** evaluate μ or a_eff for galaxies in the TAFA action as written.

## 2. Induced gravity (DGP class)

### Action structure
\[
S = -\frac{1}{16\pi G_5}\int d^5X\sqrt{-g_5}\,R_5
+ S_{\rm boundary}
- \frac{1}{16\pi G_4}\int d^4x\sqrt{-g_4}\,R_4 + S_{\rm matter}.
\]
Crossover scale:
\[
r_c \equiv \frac{1}{2}\frac{G_5}{G_4} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{M_4^2}{M_5^3}.
\]
- r ≪ r_c: 4D gravity (induced EH dominates).
- r ≫ r_c: 5D leakage (bulk EH dominates).

### Branches
- **Self-accelerating:** H ~ 1/r_c without Λ; linear ghost; observationally strained.
- **Normal:** no free self-acceleration; modified growth; needs tension-like term for late acceleration.

### Broader induced-gravity idea
Quantum fluctuations / worldvolume dynamics of a brane can generate an effective 4D Einstein–Hilbert term (Sakharov-style / Akama–Hattori type analyses). Coefficients depend on cutoff ~ inverse brane thickness — again a dimensionful input.

### TAFA contact
| Induced-gravity object | Role vs TAFA |
|------------------------|--------------|
| r_c | One IR length (same logical slot as μ^{-1} or Pathway-1 cosmological scale) |
| Induced EH on brane | Possible language for 4D effective cone-sector gravity |
| Self-accelerating DGP | **Not** adopted; Pathway 1 uses ρ_Λ explicitly |
