### Does it improve existing paper results?

**Not yet numerically.** No new SPARC residual reduction, no new galactic handedness statistic, no new cone constant has been derived from Forman flow or \(R_{\rm mid}\).

**What it can improve conceptually:**
1. **Chirality program:** gives a microscopic, residual-validated picture of how *imbalance* (weaken one pole) produces polarization and mid-hollowing — a concrete mechanism-sketch for “orientation → transport asymmetry.”
2. **Identity of microscopic objects:** soft-mode shape + texture diagnostics make “what the defect is” sharper before scaling language is used.
3. **Discipline:** separates established lattice facts from galactic narrative, reducing overclaim risk in the papers.

### What would be required to claim improvement at galactic scale
1. A derived map from lattice source variables → a galactic observable (e.g. residual chiral term in rotation curves or spin–orbit asymmetry).
2. A test on existing data (SPARC residuals, galaxy handedness catalogs).
3. Not just analogy: a coefficient or functional form fixed by the continuum cone/Callias side.

Until then: **insight and architecture**, not empirical improvement of the published SPARC/cone results.
