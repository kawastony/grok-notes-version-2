## Jacobson–Visser (causal diamonds in (A)dS)

- Causal diamonds in maximally symmetric spacetimes generally admit a **conformal Killing vector** that preserves the diamond (true Killing in static dS patch / Rindler).
- **Smarr formula** and **first law** for variations to nearby solutions relate:
  - bounding area
  - spatial volume of the maximal slice
  - cosmological constant Λ
  - matter Hamiltonian
- Total Hamiltonian generates evolution along the conformal Killing field.
- Thermodynamic reading often requires **negative temperature** for the diamond (as previously suggested for the dS static patch).
- With quantum corrections, small diamonds recover **entanglement equilibrium**: generalized entropy stationary at the maximally symmetric vacuum at fixed volume; equivalently stationarity of free conformal energy with volume not fixed.

## Bousso covariant entropy bound — applications

**Statement:** Entropy of matter on a light-sheet ≤ A/(4 ℓ_Pl²), where the light-sheet is generated by non-expanding null geodesics orthogonal to a surface of area A.

**Applications (standard literature)**
- Cosmology: satisfied in broad classes of FRW models with reasonable equations of state.
- Gravitational collapse: no known violations under the null curvature condition in classical regimes.
- de Sitter / Λ>0: together with D-bound / N-bound arguments, constrains observable entropy inside causal diamonds relative to horizon area.
- Holographic principle support: limits information by area, motivating IR cutoffs of horizon size.

**Not an application claimed here:** using Bousso saturation on a cone sector to fix a_eff or r_p in kpc.
