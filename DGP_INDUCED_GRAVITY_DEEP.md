## 1. Action

\[
S = -\frac{1}{16\pi G_5}\int d^5X\,\sqrt{-g_5}\,R_5
+ S_{\rm GHY}
- \frac{1}{16\pi G_4}\int d^4x\,\sqrt{-g_4}\,R_4

\]

Crossover:
\[
r_c = \frac{1}{2}\frac{G_5}{G_4} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{M_4^2}{M_5^3}.
\]

Below \(r_c\): induced 4D EH dominates → approximately 4D gravity.  
Above \(r_c\): bulk leakage → 5D behavior.

## 2. Modified Friedmann (brane cosmology)

Schematic empty-brane / matter form:
\[
H^2 \pm \frac{H}{r_c} = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho
\]
(sign labels the branch).

- **Self-accelerating (−):** late \(H\sim 1/r_c\) without Λ; linear **ghost**; CMB+SN+lensing tension (literature often ~4–5σ class).
- **Normal (+):** no free self-acceleration; needs tension-like term; modified growth of structure.

Junction conditions involve the extrinsic curvature of the brane (Israel-type with induced-gravity corrections).

## 3. Induced gravity more broadly

Even without a full 5D bulk Einstein term, quantum fluctuations of a thin brane (Dirac–Nambu–Goto type dynamics) can generate an effective 4D Einstein–Hilbert term. Coefficients depend on a UV cutoff ~ inverse brane thickness — again a dimensionful scale.
