# Cross-Scale Realization Conjecture

**Status:** Conjecture under the Meta-Law. Constrained so it does not make every role interchangeable.

---

## Statement

> **A pause at scale S+1 may be realized by active bridge dynamics at scale S.**  
> What is non-propagating relative to the higher frame can be actively mediating in the lower frame.

Higher-scale stillness is not empty: it can be **constituted by** subordinate lower-scale bridge activity that does not itself drive higher-scale identity change during that interval.

---

## Guardrail (required)

Lower-scale activity implements a higher-scale pause **only if**:
- it does not propagate higher-scale identity change in that interval, and
- it preserves (or only renormalizes) the higher midpoint / locked identity, and
- its average contribution to higher-scale source terms is negligible or absorbed into dressing.

Without this, the framework becomes too elastic.

## Relation to nested phase resolution

This is stronger than “roles are relative.” It says **how** a higher pause can be *implemented* by lower bridges — a vertical constitution relation, not only a relabeling.

Still: conjecture, not theorem. Needs one worked coarse-graining example before promotion.
