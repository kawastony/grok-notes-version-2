## Causal diamonds

**Definition:** Intersection of the chronological future of event p and the chronological past of event q — the spacetime region an observer can influence and be influenced by between those events.

**Special cases**
- Minkowski: ordinary double-cone diamond.
- de Sitter static patch: maximal causal diamond for a static observer; cosmological horizon is the edge.
- Rindler wedge: acceleration horizon as diamond boundary.

**Thermodynamics (Jacobson–Visser and related)**
- Diamonds in maximally symmetric spacetimes admit a conformal Killing vector preserving the diamond.
- Smarr formula and first law relate variations of: bounding area, maximal-slice volume, cosmological constant Λ, and matter Hamiltonian.
- Temperature often appears **negative** for the diamond (as suggested earlier for dS static patch).
- With quantum corrections, small diamonds recover entanglement-equilibrium: generalized entropy stationary at the maximally symmetric vacuum (fixed volume).

**Entropy**
- Area-law form S ~ A_edge/(4Gℏ) from Euclidean boundary terms / ensemble methods.
- Gibbons–Hawking dS entropy is the maximal-diamond limit.
- N-bound / D-bound (Bousso): in Λ>0, observable entropy in a diamond is constrained by horizon area structure.
