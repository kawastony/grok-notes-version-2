# Soft-mode dynamics — investigation note

**Role in TAFA:** concrete active substrate of micro bridges; carrier of G12 response and dressing variance.

---

## What soft modes are here

Near-zero eigenmodes of the lattice Dirac / Hamiltonian operator in a defect background:
- Localized near hedgehog / anti-hedgehog cores or shared along molecular tubes when cores approach.
- Count tied to topological index (protected).
- Amplitudes and spatial weight **not** protected — they flow under boosts and coupling changes.

---

## Observed / expected dynamics (lattice program)

| Feature | Behavior |
|---------|----------|
| Eigenvalues λ | Soft; move under core boost / v_coup change |
| Spatial weight | Slides between cores (G12 ≠ 0) — active exchange |
| Topological charge Q | Fixed under the same paths |
| dS / anisotropy | Responds along path; can correlate with gaps |
| Continuum limit | Softness and localization must survive refinement |

Activity = weight motion + spectral motion. Identity lock = Q / index fixed.

## Link to scale transfer

- Soft-mode weight flow is the **active bridge** at micro scale.
- After coarse-graining, contributes to **Var(δμ)** around a locked galactic μ_* (Example 1).
- Does not, under non-propagation, shift ⟨μ⟩ systematically.
- Candidate observable: residual correlation length in hierarchical SPARC inherited from soft-tube / defect-separation scale.
