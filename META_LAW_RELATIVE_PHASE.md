# Meta-Law of Relative Phase Resolution

**Status:** Meta-principle above the four laws. Formalizes the scale-dependent pause/active/bridge subtlety.

---

## Statement

> **Phase roles are scale-relative.**  
> Every system participates in both active and pause phases. What counts as active, pause, or bridge depends on the observational level and on neighboring bridge structure. A lower bridge can function as a pause relative to a higher bridge. At larger scales, phases may appear singular; at smaller scales they multiply, nest, and become relative.

The four laws operate **within any chosen scale**. This meta-law governs how phase labels change when the scale changes.

---

## What is universal vs what is scale-dependent

| Universal | Scale-dependent |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Alternation of active and pause | Number of pauses |
| Existence of bridges | Spacing / density of intervals |
| Identity selection *at some* pause | Which pause is the dominant selection gate |
| Nested hierarchy of roles | Whether a structure is “phase” or “bridge” in a given frame |

Not: “some beings have pause and others do not.”  
Yes: “all participate in alternation; resolution of alternation is relative to scale.”

---

## Nested roles (sketch)

```text
Cosmic scale:     often one major pause / pivot
                      │
Mesoscopic:       fewer but significant pauses; bridges between regimes
                      │
Microscopic:      dense nested alternations; local pauses inside larger active stretches
```

A bridge at level *n* can appear as a pause relative to level *n+1* when it mediates between higher-order phases.

---

## Implications for choice

Identity selection can occur at every scale where a pause is resolved:
- cosmic: coarse identity-choice
- meso: intermediate commitments
- micro: many local pre-choice / orientation transitions

Agency is **graded and layered**, not all-or-nothing. Lower processes may be locked into higher phase structure while still containing relative pauses.
