# 5D Randall–Sundrum contact and MOND constraints

## Randall–Sundrum (brief)

| Model | Setup | Role of scales |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| **RS1** | Two branes, finite warped extra dimension | Warp factor e^{−kπR} generates TeV from Planck; hierarchy without large volume |
| **RS2** | Single brane, infinite AdS throat | Localizes graviton; does not solve hierarchy the same way |

Metric (schematic): warped product with curvature scale k. Effective 4D Planck mass from 5D integral over the warp. IR scales on the TeV brane are redshifted.

### Analogy to TAFA cone program (provisional only)

| RS object | TAFA-like reading |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Warp factor / wall | Cone junction, A_wall, tension Λ_* |
| IR cutoff / TeV brane scale | Candidate home for μ or pause scale |
| UV Planck brane | Geometric stiffness / high scale |
| Radion / stabilized radius | Dimensionful input the no-go demands |

**Honest limit:** RS supplies a **template** for how a 5D length becomes a 4D mass/acceleration hierarchy. It does **not** by itself compute galactic a_eff or μ in the TAFA papers. Using RS language without a matched TAFA action reduction remains analogy.

## MOND constraints (SPARC-era)

| Result | Content |
|--------|--------|
| a₀ scale | ~1.2×10^{-10} m s^{-2}; Desmond (2023) a₀=(1.19±0.04±0.09)×10^{-10} |
| RAR scatter | Very small intrinsic scatter (~0.03 dex) under SPARC error model |
| Solar-system quadrupole | Tension with some MOND IFs vs RAR sharpness (literature ~few–8σ depending on sample cuts) |
| BTFR slope | Canonical MOND expects V⁴∝M; some SPARC analyses find slopes nearer ~3.5 (regime-dependent claims exist) |
| EFE | Weak evidence in some RAR fits; environment-dependent |

### Contact with TAFA Paper 24

- TAFA median a_eff ≃1.41×10^{-10} is **same order** as MOND a₀ (slightly higher).
- TAFA preferred law modulates with Σ_b^{1/7} rather than a strictly universal a₀.
- Paper 24 does not claim to beat empirical RAR forms galaxy-by-galaxy; form-level competition was near a draw in related notes.
- Pathway 1 predicts a_eff~1.39×10^{-10} (Planck-like), close to both MOND a₀ and SPARC median.

### Constraint takeaway for this program

1. Any absolute scale (MOND a₀ or TAFA a_eff) lives near 10^{-10} m s^{-2}; Pathway 1 and MOND coincide at that order.
2. Solar-system / IF sharpness tensions constrain **MOND interpolators**, not automatically the TAFA Σ_b response (different structure).
3. Do not claim TAFA solves MOND’s quadrupole problem without a dedicated calculation.
