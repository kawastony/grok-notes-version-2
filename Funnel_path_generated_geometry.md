## Construction

Centerline in effective space:
\[
X(s)\in\mathcal{M},\qquad T=\dot X/\|\dot X\|
\]

Transverse frame \{N_a\} with \(T\cdot N_a=0\).

Funnel wall:
\[
Y(s,\theta)=X(s)+r(s)\,(\cos\theta\,N_1+\sin\theta\,N_2)
\]

Fixed mouth:
\[
r(s_m)=R_0=P_0/(2\pi)
\]

Depth from path accumulation:
\[
D(s)=\int_{s_0}^{s}\alpha(u)\,\|\dot X(u)\|\,du
\]

Radius from depth (funnel sides):
\[
r(s)=R_0\,f(D(s)),\quad f\text{ decreasing}
\]

**Boxed:** The cone is swept out by the path and its transverse envelope. Depth is generated by accumulated path structure.
