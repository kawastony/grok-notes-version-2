# Zero-mode index on the cone hedgehog / activation-surface background

Tony Kawas / 14 September 2026.  
Index calculation for chiral zero-modes bound to the defect.  
Purpose: check consistency with the Callan-Harvey inflow current already derived.

---

## 1. Setup

We consider a Dirac fermion ψ coupled to the cone network through a position-dependent mass (or Yukawa) term generated by the radial mode χ:

\[
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{Yukawa}}
=
-\,g\,\chi(x)\,\bar\psi\psi
\]
(or the chiral version \(-g\chi\bar\psi i\gamma^5\psi\) according to the representation).  
The background χ interpolates from one vacuum value to another across the activation surface, or winds with topological degree N_def around the hedgehog core.

The Dirac operator in this background is
\[
D
=
i\gamma^\mu\partial_\mu
-
g\chi(x)
\]
(we work in the continuum limit outside the deep core; lattice effects only set the overall scale).

The index of interest is
\[
\mathrm{Index}(D)
=
n_+-n_-
=
dim\ker D_+
-
dim\ker D_-,
\]
where ± label the chiralities of the zero-modes.

---

## 2. Domain-wall case (activation surface)

Treat the activation surface as a flat domain wall at z = 0, with
\[
\chi(z)
\xrightarrow{z\to-\infty}\chi_-,
\qquad
\chi(z)
\xrightarrow{z\to+\infty}\chi_+,
\qquad
\chi_+\chi_-<0
\]
(so the mass term flips sign).  
This is the classic Jackiw–Rebbi setting.

### 2.1 Zero-mode equation

Look for a normalisable solution independent of x,y and of energy E = 0:
\[
\bigl(-i\gamma^3\partial_z
-
g\chi(z)\bigr)\psi(z)=0.
\]
Project onto chiral eigenstates of γ^5 (or of the appropriate wall chirality operator).  
The normalisable solution exists only for one chirality and has the profile
\[
\psi(z)
\propto
\exp\Bigl(-g\int_0^z\chi(z')\,\mathrm{d}z'\Bigr)
\times
(\text{constant spinor of definite chirality}).
\]
Provided g(χ_+ − χ_-) has the correct sign, exactly one normalisable zero-mode appears.

### 2.2 Index

\[
\mathrm{Index}(D_{\mathrm{wall}})=\pm 1.
\]
The sign is fixed by the orientation of the wall normal relative to the cone axis ĉ (the same orientation that appears in the chiral density).  
Thus a single activation surface binds one chiral zero-mode.

## 3. Hedgehog case (degree-N_def defect)

Now let χ carry the hedgehog profile already used for the topological density:
\[
\chi(r)\,\hat{\mathbf{n}}(\hat{\mathbf{x}}),
\qquad
\chi(0)=\pi\ ({\rm or\ appropriate\ core\ value}),
\qquad
\chi(\infty)=0,
\]
with
\[
N_{\mathrm{def}}
=
\frac{1}{4\pi}\int_{S^2}\mathbf{n}\cdot(\partial_\theta\mathbf{n}\times\partial_\varphi\mathbf{n})\,\mathrm{d}\Omega
\in\mathbb{Z}.
\]

### 3.1 Callan–Harvey / Jackiw–Rossi index theorem

For a Dirac fermion in three spatial dimensions coupled to a hedgehog of topological degree N_def, the index theorem states
\[
\mathrm{Index}(D_{\mathrm{hedgehog}})
=
N_{\mathrm{def}}.
\]
(The precise factor depends on whether the Yukawa coupling is scalar or pseudoscalar and on the representation of the gamma matrices; the absolute value is |N_def| and the sign tracks the orientation of the map.)

### 3.2 Sketch of the argument

- Far from the core the fermion is massive and can be integrated out.  
- The topological density of the hedgehog acts as a source for the axial current.  
- By the Atiyah–Singer index theorem (or by direct solution of the Dirac equation in the hedgehog background) the number of normalisable zero-modes equals the winding number of the map S² → S².  
- For the standard degree-1 hedgehog one obtains a single zero-mode of definite chirality (the classic Jackiw–Rossi zero-mode of the monopole).

Thus
\[
\mathrm{Index}(D)
=
N_{\mathrm{def}}.
\]

## 4. Matching to the inflow current

The Callan-Harvey inflow current derived previously has divergence
\[
\partial_\mu J^\mu_{\mathrm{inflow}}
=
\frac{\kappa}{4\pi}\,N_{\mathrm{def}}\,(\mathbf{E}\cdot\mathbf{B})\Big|_{\mathrm{defect}}.
\]

On the other hand, the anomaly of a single left-handed Weyl fermion in an electromagnetic background is
\[
\partial_\mu J^\mu_5
=
\frac{1}{8\pi^2}\,\mathbf{E}\cdot\mathbf{B}
\]
(standard normalisation with ħ = c = 1; factors of e restored by A → eA).  
For |N_def| zero-modes of the same chirality the anomaly is multiplied by N_def.

Comparing the two expressions fixes the relative normalisation between the continuum χ-gradient and the canonical axial anomaly.  
With the coefficient κ already locked by R_cone, the matching condition becomes
\[
\frac{\kappa}{4\pi}
=
\frac{|N_{\mathrm{def}}|}{8\pi^2}\times(\text{normalisation of }\chi)
\]
or an equivalent rearrangement once the precise Yukawa normalisation and the units of χ are chosen.  
The important point is that both sides are proportional to the same integer N_def; the topological protection guarantees that the cancellation remains exact once the overall factor is fixed.

## 5. Summary of the index

| Background | Index | Chirality |
|---|---|---|
| Single activation-surface domain wall | ±1 | fixed by wall orientation relative to ĉ |
| Hedgehog of degree N_def | N_def | sign tracks orientation of the map |

In both cases the index equals the topological invariant that already appears in the divergence of the inflow current.  
Therefore the Callan-Harvey cancellation is consistent at the level of the index theorem.

## 6. What is established vs still open

**Established**
- Existence of chiral zero-modes bound to the activation surface (index ±1).  
- Existence of chiral zero-modes bound to the hedgehog (index = N_def).  
- Proportionality of the zero-mode anomaly to the same integer that multiplies the inflow divergence.  
- Consistency of the geometric setup with the Callan-Harvey mechanism.

**Still open**
- Precise numerical factor relating the continuum χ to the canonical axial anomaly (requires a definite normalisation of the Yukawa coupling and of the kinetic term of χ).  
- Stability of the zero-modes once the full network dynamics (including the residual chiral term) are restored.  
- Explicit construction of the zero-mode wave-function on the lattice version of the cone network.

## 7. Compact statement

The Dirac operator in the background of the cone radial mode χ has
\[
\mathrm{Index}(D)
=
N_{\mathrm{def}}
\]
for a hedgehog of topological degree N_def (and Index = ±1 for a single activation-surface domain wall).  
This index multiplies the chiral anomaly of the bound zero-modes and is precisely cancelled by the divergence of the Callan-Harvey inflow current already derived from the same geometric objects.  
The microscopic anomaly structure is therefore consistent.
