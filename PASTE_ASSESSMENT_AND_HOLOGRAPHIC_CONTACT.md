## 3. Causal diamond entropy

A **causal diamond** is the intersection of the chronological future of p and past of q — the region an observer can probe between two events.

- In de Sitter, the static patch is a maximal causal diamond; Gibbons–Hawking entropy S = A/(4Gℏ) for the cosmological horizon.
- Jacobson–Visser et al.: gravitational thermodynamics of causal diamonds in (A)dS — Smarr formula, first law involving area, volume, Λ, matter Hamiltonian; often interpreted with **negative temperature** for the diamond.
- Euclidean methods recover area-law entropy from boundary terms at the diamond edge; ensemble interpretations exist for diamond ensembles.

**N-bound / D-bound** (Bousso): in Λ>0 spacetimes, observable entropy in a causal diamond is bounded in terms of horizon area differences.
