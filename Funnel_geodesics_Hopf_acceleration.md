## 2. When are the sides geodesics?

**Ideal cone:** generators (straight lines apex–rim in the unrolled plane) **are** geodesics of the cone metric.

**General swept surface** from centerline X(s) and radius r(s):
- Side curves at fixed θ are **generator curves** of the ruled/swept surface.
- They are geodesics only if the induced metric makes their geodesic curvature vanish.

For a **surface of revolution** (axisymmetric funnel), Clairaut’s relation holds:
\[
r\,\cos\theta = \text{const}
\]
along geodesics (r = distance from axis, θ = angle to the parallel). Meridians (θ = π/2 to parallel, i.e. pure “down-funnel”) with c = 0 are geodesics.

**Safe claim:** sides are natural generator curves; in the ideal cone / pure meridian case they are geodesics; in the general path-generated case call them geodesic-like transport channels until the induced metric is specified and checked.
