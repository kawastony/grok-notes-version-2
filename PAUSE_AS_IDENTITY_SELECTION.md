# Pause phase as identity selection

**Status:** Interpretive upgrade of Law 1. Implicit in midpoint/pause language; now stated explicitly.

Canonical freeze on absolute scale / one-input boundary unchanged.

---

## Core statement

> **The pause phase is the identity-selection phase.**  
> It is the interval in which the system is not yet committed to propagation, but can select the orientation that later propagates, balances, and appears in observables.

Pause is not only where identity is *preserved*. It is where identity is *chosen, ratified, or locked*.

---

## Revised law language (within any fixed scale)

| Law | Revised reading |
|-----|-----------------|
| **1 — Identity selection** | At the pause, the system selects and locks the identity that remains invariant through subsequent deformation. |
| **2 — Propagation** | What is chosen at the pause unfolds through the medium as sourced deformation. |
| **3 — Exchange** | The chosen identity generates compensating sector balances as the system expresses itself. |
| **4 — Bridge** | The large-scale observable is the asymptotic readout of the identity selected at the pause. |

Sequence: **pause → choose → propagate → exchange → bridge readout.**

## Equal intervals

Framework papers and notes describe **alternation** of active and pause with characteristic spacing. Equal (or regular) intervals between pause and active are a structural motif, especially in microscopic/quantum treatments. Cosmic and galactic layers often emphasize a coarser pause (e.g. one major crossover / pause radius), not a dense equal-interval lattice — consistent with scale-relative resolution (see META_LAW_RELATIVE_PHASE.md).

## Choice and suffering (philosophical layer)

- Pause: capacity to select orientation rather than only carry prior scars or external force.
- Active: expression of the chosen identity; intensity of consequence (including suffering) can increase with how that identity binds the system in the active stretch.
- This is **interpretive**, not a derived dynamical equation. It must not be used to override empirical tests (SPARC, cosmology) or the one-input no-go.

---

## What this does not claim

- Not that every local fluctuation is a free will event.
- Not that SI scales are derived from “choice.”
- Not that (w₀, wₐ) or 12.1 kpc are forced by identity-selection language alone.
