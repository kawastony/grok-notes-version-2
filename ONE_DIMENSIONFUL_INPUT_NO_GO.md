# One-dimensionful-input boundary (no-go lemma)

## Statement

**At least one external dimensionful input is necessary** to obtain an SI acceleration (or an SI length such as μ⁻¹) from the cone-sector geometric content currently written in the TAFA papers.

Safer label: **dimensional boundary statement / no-go lemma** — not a fully formalized theorem over all possible actions unless the action class is specified exhaustively.

---

## Assumptions (current scope)

Internal quantities treated as available without external scales:

| Quantity | Type |
|----------|------|
| Angles ψ₁, ψ₂ (35°, 70°) | Dimensionless |
| Q=√2, φ, R_cone=√6/φ | Dimensionless O(1) |
| κ₀, C_ang | Dimensionless O(1) |
| ν₀ (Legendre), Δ_X, n_eff, γ | Dimensionless |
| Σ_b/Σ_ref | Dimensionless ratio |
| Topological integers (N_wind, n_eff, …) | Dimensionless |

Any algebraic combination of these is dimensionless (or a pure number). Acceleration has dimensions L T⁻².

---

## Conclusion

To reach SI a_eff one must import **at least one** of:
- ρ_Λ or H₀ (Pathway 1 class),
- a UV/compactification scale (M₅, k, wall thickness δ),
- a bulk-field VEV or mass (Goldberger–Wise type),
- a lattice spacing matched to SI,
- or an empirical calibration (SPARC median).

The lemma asserts **necessity**, not uniqueness of the input.

---

## Role of GW and induced gravity

| Template | Role |
|----------|------|
| Goldberger–Wise | Stabilizes a warped length once bulk mass / VEVs are supplied — **handles** the one input |
| DGP / induced EH | Introduces crossover r_c or brane thickness — again **handles** one length |

They are structural templates for *how* a dimensionful scale enters, not failures of the cone program.

---

## What the no-go does not kill

Relative structure: shallow Σ_b response, operator dimensions, geometric O(1) family, residual diagnostics. Those remain well-posed without fixing SI a_eff.
