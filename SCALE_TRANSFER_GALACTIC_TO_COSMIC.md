# Scale-transfer example 2: galactic active bridge → cosmic pause

**Status:** Second worked instantiation of Cross-Scale Realization. Realization claim, not origin claim. Does not derive ω_* from galaxies alone.

---

## 1. Scales chosen

| Level | Scale | Variables |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **S** | Galactic / mesoscopic | δμ_i, regime bridges, ensemble bridge current j_g (i over galaxies) |
| **S+1** | Cosmic | ω_*, δω |

**Claim:** Active galactic bridge dynamics realize a cosmic pause around ω_* without driving systematic cosmic identity change.

---

## 2. Lower-scale active

Galaxies remain dynamically active:
- regime crossing and midpoint dressing
- compressive/tensile exchange
- residual bridge currents j_g
- environmental / Σ_b reorganization

Schematic:
\[
\dot{\delta\mu}_i = F_i(\delta\mu_i,\Sigma_{b,i},j_{g,i};\theta_i)+\eta_i.
\]

---

## 3. Higher-scale pause

Cosmic pause (Case B):
\[
|\delta\omega|<\varepsilon_\omega,\qquad \langle\dot\omega\rangle\approx 0
\]
— identity locked; no endpoint switching at cosmic scale; no net drift of ω_* over the interval.

Pause in **cosmic identity propagation**, not emptiness below.

---

## 4. Coarse-graining map

\[
\omega(T)=\mathcal{C}_{g\to c}[\{\mu_i,\delta\mu_i,j_{g,i}\}]
\]
Minimal schematic:
\[
\omega = \omega_* + \frac{1}{V_H}\sum_i w_i\,\delta\mu_i + \frac{1}{V_H}\sum_i \alpha_i j_{g,i}.
\]
Galactic activity → **fluctuation dressing** around ω_*, not necessarily mean drift.

---

## 5. Non-propagation condition

\[
\langle\mathcal{C}_{g\to c}[F_i]\rangle\approx 0
\quad\Leftrightarrow\quad
|\dot\omega|\ll \tau_c^{-1}|\omega_*|.
\]

Mechanisms:
- statistical cancellation / isotropy at horizon scale
- equal/opposite compressive–tensile exchange
- timescale separation τ_g ≪ τ_c
- topological / midpoint locking of ω_* (Pathway 1 input held fixed)

---

## 6. Invariant preserved

\[
\mathcal{I}_c = \omega_* = \text{const.}
\]
(and the single cosmological input scale, e.g. √(Gρ_Λ), if used). Lower invariants may evolve; they do not unlock higher identity.

---

## 7. Role transformation

> Galactic bridge activity is active in its native frame, but **pause-realizing** at cosmic scale: it contributes bounded dressing around the cosmic midpoint without driving higher-scale identity propagation.

---

## 8. Observable signature

\[
\langle\dot\omega\rangle\approx 0,\qquad
\mathrm{Var}(\omega)\sim\mathcal{C}^{(2)}_{g\to c}[\delta\mu_i,j_{g,i}].
\]

- Stable cosmic midpoint (Pathway 1 scale held).
- Structured, not featureless, residual dressing.
- Possible inheritance of correlation scales from galactic ensemble statistics.
- No large shift of ω_* from population-level galactic activity alone.

---

## 9. Falsifiers

- Coarse-grained galactic activity induces systematic drift of ω.
- No stable cosmic midpoint identifiable.
- Residual cosmic dressing unbounded or pure white when structure is predicted.
- Cancellation requires fine-tuned many-parameter inputs.
- No plausible inheritance from galactic ensemble into cosmic residual structure.

---

## 10. Limits

Realization claim only: once a cosmic midpoint exists, galactic bridges may realize its pause. Does **not** derive ω_* from galactic physics, remove the one-input boundary, or replace cosmology fits. \(\mathcal{C}_{g\to c}\) still schematic.
