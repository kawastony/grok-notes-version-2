**Operators:** γ₅ = β⊗I, and mass-texture β⊗(τ·n̂(x)) (the successful soft diagnostic).

\[

## Chirality probability field (practical definition)

For soft modes, the useful field is **texture-weighted density**:

\[
\rho_T(x) \equiv \chi_T(x)
\quad\text{(signed; soft modes live at }\chi_T<0\text{)}
\]

or the polarization-masked density

\[
\rho^{\chi}_T(x) = \rho(x)\,\frac{\chi_T(x)}{|\chi_T|_{\rm typ}}
\]

**Not** a left/right probability in the continuum γ₅ sense — a **defect-texture alignment probability / density**.

Both cores carry the **same** soft texture sign (anti-alignment). The molecular pair is **chirally co-aligned in texture**, spatially shared — not a γ₅ dipole between cores.

---

## Link to elongation

- A and texture dipole \|D_T\|: mild positive correlation (r≈0.5).  
- A and QT / ST: weak.  
- Elongation is **not** driven by opposite-core γ₅ structure in this data.

A chirality probability field can **weight** the second-moment sums defining A (e.g. moments of ρ only where χ_T < 0), but that is a refinement, not a new gravity mechanism.

---

## Compact statement

Local mass-texture chirality is uniformly negative on soft modes and on both defect cores (ST>0 always). γ₅ local structure is weak. Soft “chirality probability” is best defined via texture alignment, not opposite-core γ₅. Mild link between texture dipole and anisotropy A; no tight gap–chirality law. Useful for weighted elongation diagnostics later; not evidence for interior gravity cycles.
