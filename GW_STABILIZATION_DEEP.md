## 1. Why the radion exists

RS1 metric (schematic):
\[
ds^2 = e^{-2k|y|}\eta_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu + dy^2,
\]
with branes at the orbifold fixed points. The proper inter-brane distance is a modulus field (radion). Without a potential, its VEV is undetermined and the hierarchy is unstable under quantum corrections.

---

## 2. GW mechanism (structure)

Introduce bulk scalar Φ with:
- bulk mass term \(m^2\Phi^2\),
- boundary potentials that prefer different VEVs \(v_h\) (UV) and \(v_v\) (IR).

For small \(\varepsilon \approx m^2/(4k^2)\), the effective 4D potential for the radion (modulus coordinate related to \(r_c\)) has a minimum at
\[
k r_c \approx \frac{4}{\pi}\frac{k^2}{m^2}\ln\frac{v_h}{v_v}.
\]
O(1) ratios \(v_h/v_v\) and \(m/k\sim 0.1\) give \(kr_c\sim 12\) — the classic mild-parameter hierarchy solution.

Backreaction on the metric can be included; it shifts the precise minimum and radion mass/couplings but does not remove the need for the bulk scalar (or equivalent bulk dynamics).

## 3. Phenomenology of the stabilized radion

- Couplings to SM fields on the IR brane are fixed by 4D covariance and set by the **IR scale** (often written \(\Lambda_\phi\sim{\rm TeV}\)), not the Planck scale.
- If the bulk mass is small (as needed for large separation), the radion tends to be **lighter than the lightest KK modes** → possible first collider signature of the warped model.
- Production/decay resemble a Higgs-like scalar with model-dependent trace-anomaly enhancements (e.g. to gluons).
